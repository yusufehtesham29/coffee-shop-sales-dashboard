Coffee Shop Sales Analysis - Key Insights

Executive Summary

Analysis of 149,117 transactions from January-June 2023 across 3 coffee shop locations, revealing $698,812 in total revenue with an average transaction value of $4.69.

Top Findings

Product Performance
Coffee Dominates Sales Coffee category: $269,952 (39% of total revenue) Tea category: $196,406 (28% of total revenue) Bakery category: $82,316 (12% of total revenue)

Best-Selling Products:

Barista Espresso - $8,200+
Brewed Chai Tea - $7,500+
Brewed Herbal Tea - $6,800+
Green Beans - $6,500+
House Blend Beans - $6,200+
Recommendation: Focus inventory and promotions on coffee products, especially Barista Espresso.

Time-Based Patterns
Peak Sales Hours: 8:00-11:00 AM - Morning rush generates $20,000+ daily After 2:00 PM - Sales decline significantly After 6:00 PM - Minimal sales (less than $2,000)

Busiest Days: Thursday: $9,106 average Friday: $8,648 average Tuesday: $8,173 average

Slowest Days: Wednesday: $6,449 Sunday: $7,358

Recommendation: Schedule maximum staff 8-11 AM, especially Thursday-Friday Consider reduced hours after 6 PM Run promotions on Wednesday to boost sales

Weekday vs Weekend Analysis
Weekday Performance: Average daily sales: $8,100 Transaction volume: Higher Peak hours: 8-11 AM (commuters)

Weekend Performance: Average daily sales: $7,700 Transaction volume: Lower More distributed throughout the day

Recommendation: Weekday focus: Quick service, grab-and-go items Weekend focus: Relaxed atmosphere, food pairings
